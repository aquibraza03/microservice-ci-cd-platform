# Customer communication and status page
## Purpose
Coordinate customer-cluster disconnection and incident communication.
## Symptoms
Confirmed user impact, repeated target disconnect, or incident severity requiring updates.
## User impact
State the affected journey, scope, and known workaround without speculation.
## Required access
Communication owner and incident commander; status publishing permission is separate.
## Diagnostic queries
Use aggregated tenant-safe metrics and confirmed incident timeline; never expose another tenant.
## Safe mitigation
Publish acknowledged impact and next update time; provide safe workaround approved by engineering.
## Recovery steps
Update through mitigation, monitoring, and resolved states; contact specifically affected organizations through approved channels.
## Escalation criteria
Follow SEV model in this directory; Security approves security-incident wording.
## Recovery verification
Engineering verifies the user journey; communication owner confirms final update and support linkage.
## Evidence preservation
Preserve published messages, approvals, timestamps, and incident ID.
## Follow-up
Post-incident review and customer summary when appropriate.
