# SLI and SLO catalogue

All targets are **provisional** until four weeks of representative production traffic
and measurement-quality review exist. Platform failures are server errors, timeouts,
dependency failures, lost accepted work, or incorrect state caused by the platform.
Authenticated user validation/policy denials are included in traffic denominators but
not platform-failure numerators unless the platform misclassified or caused them.
Missing data is never treated as success. It produces `unknown`; separate telemetry and
component-absence alerts cover measurement loss.

Default window is rolling 30 days, operational retention is 30 days for metrics, and
the error-budget policy freezes risky releases at 100% budget consumption, requires an
owner review at 50%, and pages on the documented fast/slow multi-window burn rates.

## Error-budget usage

- Fast burn: 14.4x the 0.1% availability budget for 5m, sustained 2m.
- Slow burn: 6x for 1h, sustained 15m. Production should add paired 1h/6h and
  6h/3d recording rules after representative traffic validates thresholds.
- Validation, authorization and policy failures remain observable result categories even
  when excluded from a platform-availability failure numerator.
- Low traffic and missing metrics are shown as unknown, never coerced to 100% success.
